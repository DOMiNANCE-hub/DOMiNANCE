# Quaternary structure and cooperative protein assemblies

## Learning goals
Identify subunit composition, distinguish association from one-chain folding and explain how interactions between chains can produce capabilities absent from isolated components.

## More than one chain creates another structural level
Quaternary structure describes the association and spatial arrangement of multiple polypeptide subunits in a functional assembly. A monomeric protein has one polypeptide chain and no multi-subunit quaternary level in this usage. A homodimer contains two equivalent chain types, written α2 when α labels that type. A heterodimer contains two different types, such as αβ. These α and β labels denote chain identities, not α-helical versus β-sheet secondary structure.

A chain may contain both helices and sheets regardless of its subunit label. Equally, a single multidomain chain does not become multimeric merely because its parts perform several functions. Interchain contacts can use hydrophobic packing, hydrogen bonds, electrostatics and sometimes disulfides. Quaternary assembly does not require a new peptide bond between the chains.

## Haemoglobin is a useful comparison with myoglobin
Typical adult haemoglobin A has two α and two β chains, α2β2, each with a haem group able to bind one oxygen molecule. Its four-subunit assembly permits communication between sites. Oxygen binding can shift the conformational population towards states with higher affinity at remaining sites: positive cooperativity. This does not mean every site is always filled, that the first site changes the number of chains, or that oxygen is consumed as a substrate by the binding event.

Myoglobin is a single-chain haem protein with one oxygen-binding site and does not show the same multi-site positive cooperativity. It serves an oxygen-binding role in muscle; haemoglobin primarily transports oxygen in blood. The comparison shows why a shared prosthetic group does not imply identical organisation or physiological function. Detailed binding equations and clinical interpretation require additional context; no oxygen-delivery decision is inferred from this model alone.

## Worked assembly inventory
A hypothetical α2β2 complex has four chains but only two chain types. If each α has 100 residues and each β has 80, total residues=2×100+2×80=360. For four separate ordinary linear backbones, peptide bonds=360−4=356. There are four N and four C termini before modifications. Subunit association does not change these counts.

If the complex dissociates while every chain remains folded, its quaternary structure has changed without requiring loss of each chain's tertiary structure. Conversely, one chain can partly unfold while the assembly remains associated. Measurements of total mass, chain sequence and function are needed to distinguish these cases.

## When sequence and assembly become biologically important
A familiar haemoglobin example replaces a glutamate with valine in the mature β chain of HbS. Replacing a charged side chain with a hydrophobic one changes surface chemistry and can promote abnormal intermolecular association in the deoxygenated state. This is different from simply saying that every mutant molecule is permanently unfolded. It also differs from the normal association of four subunits into one haemoglobin molecule. This example illustrates a mechanism; genotype, environment and clinical context govern disease expression.

Collagen's triple-chain assembly, antibody chain organisation and enzyme complexes provide other examples of useful associations. They differ in geometry and linkage chemistry. Cooperation may involve communication, positioning or complementary activities; being multimeric alone does not prove positive ligand-binding cooperativity.

## Short concept checks
- How many chains and chain types are in α2β2? **Answer:** Four chains and two types, assuming α and β label distinct polypeptides.
- Does dissociation of an oligomer prove every peptide bond has broken? **Answer:** No. Interchain contacts can be disrupted while each backbone remains intact.

## Key takeaways
Count chains separately from domains and secondary structures. Association can enable communication and function, but each claimed cooperative mechanism needs evidence.
