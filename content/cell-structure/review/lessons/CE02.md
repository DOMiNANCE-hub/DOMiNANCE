## Trace each fraction through two spins
The lecturer's first example spin yields a pellet enriched in unbroken cells/heavy nuclei and a supernatant containing material not yet sedimented. The **supernatant**, not the unchanged original tube, supplies the next higher-force spin. That later pellet is enriched in mitochondria, lysosomes and microbodies in the example.

| Stage | Keep track of | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Homogenate | All disrupted material | Mixed starting preparation |
| First pellet | Larger/fast-sedimenting components | Enrichment, not proof of identity |
| First supernatant | Remaining suspended material | Input for a later spin |
| Later pellet | Additional sedimented components | Still potentially mixed |

Size, density and shape influence sedimentation, so a pellet is not defined by size alone and is not automatically pure. Appropriate marker assays can test what was recovered. Rotor radius matters to force, so equal rpm on different rotors need not give equal ×g.
**Optional check:** Why is the phrase “mitochondrial fraction” insufficient proof of purity? **Answer:** Other particles can co-sediment; identity/enrichment must be tested with suitable evidence.
