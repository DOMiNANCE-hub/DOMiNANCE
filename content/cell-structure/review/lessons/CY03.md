## Structural direction is not electrical charge
A repeated head-to-tail arrangement of α/β-tubulin creates different ends. The plus/minus labels describe those structurally distinct ends, not a positive/negative electrical potential like a membrane voltage. Track orientation helps organise directional transport.
Dynamic instability describes switching between growth and shrinkage. Assembly, a stabilising GTP-rich end region and hydrolysis-dependent stability explain why a track can persist yet be reorganised quickly. Catastrophe is rapid shortening; rescue is renewed growth. Neither term means that the cell has permanently destroyed every microtubule.
**Optional check:** Why can blocking disassembly interfere with mitosis even though microtubules remain present? **Answer:** A functioning spindle needs controlled dynamics and rearrangement, not merely a fixed amount of polymer.
