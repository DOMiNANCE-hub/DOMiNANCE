## Link energy, acidity and recycling
ATP-driven H⁺ transport maintains an acidic lysosomal lumen that supports acid hydrolases. The pump establishes conditions for digestion; it is not itself the enzyme that hydrolyses every cargo molecule. Compartmentalisation also separates that environment from the cytosol.
For a damaged intracellular organelle, autophagic delivery and lysosomal breakdown recycle usable components. For material taken up from outside, endocytic/phagocytic pathways supply cargo. Both can involve lysosomal digestion, but cargo origin distinguishes autophagy from heterophagy. Autolysis is a different context of self-digestion, especially after cell death.
**Optional check:** Does an acid hydrolase in a compartment prove that all material there will be completely digested? **Answer:** No. Enzyme specificity, substrate accessibility and function matter; undigested material may remain in residual compartments.
