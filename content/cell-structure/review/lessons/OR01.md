## Couple oxidation to peroxide control
An oxidase can transfer reducing equivalents to oxygen and generate H₂O₂. Catalase then removes that reactive product. The compartment's importance is not simply that it contains “digestive enzymes”: oxidative metabolism and peroxide control differ from lysosomal acid hydrolysis.
For the displayed catalase reaction, two H₂O₂ molecules supply four hydrogen atoms and four oxygen atoms; two H₂O plus one O₂ conserve both. The products illustrate chemical conversion rather than disappearance of the oxygen atoms.
Do not infer a species-specific enzyme from the organelle's name alone. The consolidated human uricase clarification distinguishes the lecturer's cross-organism list from human physiology; it does not imply that humans lack peroxisomes or catalase.
**Optional check:** Why is peroxide production not evidence that a peroxisome is malfunctioning? **Answer:** Peroxide can be a normal oxidative product; coordinated removal helps limit damaging accumulation.
