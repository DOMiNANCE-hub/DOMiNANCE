## Choose what must survive the preparation
If the goal is intact organelles, membrane disruption must release them without unnecessarily destroying their own membranes. A supportive iso-osmotic environment and appropriately controlled mechanical treatment serve that purpose. A method intended to recover soluble proteins may tolerate or require more extensive lysis. No disruption method is universally best for every cell and analytical goal.
The lecture's hypotonic facilitator therefore does not contradict using iso-osmotic medium for organelle protection: the intended stage and goal differ. Ask what should be disrupted and what should remain intact before choosing a condition.
