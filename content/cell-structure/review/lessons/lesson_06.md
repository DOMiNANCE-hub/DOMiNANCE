## Apply the resolution relationship
At λ=560 nm, compare NA=0.70 with NA=1.40 using r=0.61λ/NA. The predicted limits are 488 nm and 244 nm. Higher NA has halved the minimum separation, so it improves resolution even if the displayed magnification is unchanged. A smaller limit means better resolving ability; a larger number is not automatically better.

Compare lenses at a stated magnification rather than assume that the higher magnification always has the larger NA. Actual specimen preparation, aberrations and illumination also constrain practical detail.
**Optional check:** With λ fixed, what happens to r when NA increases from 0.70 to 1.40? **Answer:** r halves; at 560 nm the example changes from 488 nm to 244 nm.
