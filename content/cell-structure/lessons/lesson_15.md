# Lesson 15 — Dehydration, Clearing and Embedding

### Why embedding is needed

Soft biological material often lacks the strength needed for thin sectioning. **Embedding** gives the specimen support so it can be cut without collapsing or tearing. The supporting medium depends on the required section thickness.

### Paraffin wax preparation

A typical wax-embedding sequence is:

1. **Fix** the specimen.
2. **Dehydrate** it through increasing concentrations of ethanol, ending with absolute alcohol.
3. **Clear** it with **xylene**, which replaces the alcohol and is compatible with molten wax.
4. **Infiltrate** the specimen with molten **paraffin wax**, replacing the clearing agent.
5. Allow the wax to solidify to provide support for sectioning.

**Clearing** is the replacement of the dehydrating alcohol with a solvent such as xylene; it is not simply washing the specimen.

### Epoxy resin preparation

For very thin sections, a harder medium such as **epoxy resin (e.g., Araldite)** provides greater support. A resin solvent such as **propylene oxide** may replace xylene; after infiltration, the resin is heated to **polymerise** (harden through a chemical reaction).

For relatively thick plant sections, material such as **raw carrot or unripe pawpaw** may provide temporary mechanical support.

### Example processing schedule

The following is a *specific example from the teaching material*, not a universal laboratory protocol:

| Stage | Reagent | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Dehydration | 70% alcohol | 60 min |
| Dehydration | 90% alcohol | 45 min |
| Dehydration | Absolute alcohol | 45 min |
| Dehydration | Absolute alcohol | 45 min |
| Dehydration | Absolute alcohol | 60 min |
| Clearing | Xylene | 60 min × 3 |
| Infiltration | Paraffin wax | 30 min, then 60 min, then 90 min |

**Concept Connections:** Water removal → alcohol dehydration; alcohol removal → xylene clearing; xylene replacement → wax infiltration; harder resin → support for ultrathin electron-microscopy sections.

**Slide Essentials:** Graded ethanol up to **100%**, **xylene** for wax processing, **propylene oxide** for the resin workflow, **paraffin wax** versus **epoxy resin/Araldite**, and the specific sequence and times above.

**Optional check:** What is the role of xylene in wax embedding? *It replaces alcohol and permits subsequent infiltration by molten paraffin wax.*
